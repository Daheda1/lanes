1Who is responsible

lanes, CVR [number], Aalborg, is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@lanes.dk, +45 30 36 00 86.

2What we collect and why

  • When you contact us, through the form or by email: your name, email, phone number, company and what you write. We use it to answer you and to set up a call. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR article 6(1)(f)).
  • When you become a customer: the names and contact details of the people we work with. We use them to deliver what we agreed. Legal basis: the agreement (article 6(1)(b)).
  • Invoices and bookkeeping: what the law requires us to keep. Legal basis: legal obligation (article 6(1)(c)).

We don’t ask for more than we need, and we don’t use your data for anything else, such as newsletters, unless you ask us to.

3The website

The website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses for security. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads.

4Who we share it with

We use a few suppliers to run the business, such as our email and hosting providers and our accounting system. They only process data on our instructions. We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Some suppliers are based outside the EU. When data goes there, it is protected by the EU’s standard contractual clauses or a similar legal basis.

5Data we handle for customers

When we work in a customer’s systems, the customer is responsible for that data and we process it on their behalf, under a data processing agreement signed before we start.

6How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to an agreement: deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer contact details: for as long as we work together, and up to 3 years after.
  • Bookkeeping: 5 years, as Danish law requires.

7Your rights

You can ask to see the data we have about you, have it corrected or deleted, limit how we use it, object to our use of it, and receive it in a usable format. Write to hello@lanes.dk and we answer within a month.

If you’re not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

We update this policy when something changes. The date above shows the latest version.
