Questions

What if you don't find anything worth fixing?

Then you don't pay for the two days, and you keep the as-is drawing. We'd rather tell you that than sell you something you don't need.

Who needs to be in the room?

The people who actually do the work: typically a couple of sellers, someone from sales ops or customer service, and whoever handles pricing or invoicing. Nobody needs to be there for two full days. Most people need a few hours.

What happens after the two days?

You get the as-is and to-be swimlanes and the time estimate. Then you decide: build the to-be with us, build it yourselves, or leave it. There's no obligation to continue.

Which tools do you work with?

The common CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Microsoft Dynamics), Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, Slack and Teams, and the major AI model providers. If you run something else, we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

What happens to our data?

During the analysis we only need to see how your systems are used, not export anything. If we build, we work inside your existing systems and agreements, use enterprise AI services that don't train on your data, and sign a data processing agreement first.
