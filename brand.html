1 Essence

lanes finds the friction in a sales process and removes it.

The name
From swimlanes: one lane per role, and every step in the lane of whoever does it. Always written in lowercase: lanes.
The offer
Two days, two swimlanes, one estimate. Then we build the to-be and keep it running.
Who it’s for
Small and mid-sized B2B sales teams, starting in Denmark.
Personality
Calm, practical and honest, with a little dry humour. A senior person who has seen the mess before and isn’t impressed by hype.
Point of view
Fixing the process is always better than adding AI.

How it worksThe lanes method

  • Sentence case everywhere. Capitals only for the small tracked kicker above dark heroes.
  • No monospace. Not for labels, not for numbers, not for “technical” flavour.
  • Numbers in tables line up. Use tabular figures.

5 Voice

Write like a senior person explaining something to a busy colleague: plainly, concretely, and without selling. Short sentences. Real examples.

  • Concrete beats clever. Minutes, steps, roles and real situations, not abstractions.
  • Honest about limits. Say what we won’t do and when something isn’t worth it.
  • A little dry humour is welcome. “Only Kim knows how it works.”
  • “We” for lanes, “I” when Danni signs an article.
  • Calls to action work without context. One per view, and it says “Book a call”.

8 Motion

Slow, calm and meaningful. Things move the way a process moves: along a line, one step after another.

lanes

On scroll, the name fades and the space closes, leaving the mark.

  • What moves: lines drawing themselves, a clock hand, beads along a lane, the logo collapsing.
  • What doesn’t: text fading in on scroll, bouncing buttons, anything that loops fast.
  • Always pausable, and still when the visitor asks for reduced motion.

9 What we don’t do

Learned the hard way while building the site.

  • No dark dashboards or flowchart interfaces. They look generic and generated.
  • No monospace “AI font”.
  • No long hero text. One headline, one line, one button.
  • Don’t lead with “no cure, no pay”. Quality first; the guarantee is a footnote.
  • No stacked calls to action. One per view, saying “Book a call”.
  • No invented numbers or case studies. Examples are labelled as examples.
  • No hidden text for AI or search engines. Everything we say is visible to people.
