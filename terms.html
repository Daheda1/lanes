1Scope

  1. These general terms apply to everything lanes, CVR [number], (“lanes”, “we”) delivers to a customer (“you”): process analysis, advice, building and running automation and AI tools, and support (the “services”).
  2. An “agreement” is any accepted offer, signed contract or written confirmation by email that describes services we deliver to you.
  3. We only work with businesses. These terms are not written for consumers.

2What applies first

  1. If documents say different things, they apply in this order: (1) a data processing agreement, (2) the agreement and its appendices, including the accepted offer, (3) these general terms.
  2. Software from third parties, for example a CRM, an AI service or a form service, is always covered by that supplier’s own licence terms. You may need to accept those terms to use it.

3The work we do

  1. We do the work with care, in line with good practice, and with people who know what they are doing.
  2. We commit to doing the work well, not to a specific result, unless the agreement says otherwise. A process analysis shows where the time goes; how much time you actually get back also depends on decisions you make.
  3. We are responsible for our own work. We are not responsible for work your other suppliers or your own people do, or for changes they make to what we built.

4The two-day guarantee

  1. If a two-day process analysis doesn’t turn up anything worth fixing, you don’t pay for the two days. You keep the as-is drawing.
  2. We agree together at the walk-through whether that is the case. If you think it is, tell us in writing within 5 working days of the walk-through.
  3. The guarantee covers the price of the two days. It does not cover travel or other expenses agreed in the offer.

5Your part

  1. You give us access to the people, information and systems the work needs, in the time agreed. For an analysis we need to see how systems are used; we don’t need to export anything.
  2. You make sure that information you give us is correct, and that you have the licences and permissions needed for us to work in your systems.
  3. You keep backups of your own systems and data, unless we have agreed in writing that we do it.
  4. If the work is delayed because something on your side is missing, deadlines move accordingly, and extra time we spend because of it may be invoiced at our hourly rate.

6Moving or cancelling dates

  1. You can move an agreed date, for example the two days of an analysis, once and free of charge, if you tell us at least 10 working days before.
  2. If you move or cancel with less notice, we may invoice 50% of the price for the days affected, unless we can use the time for other work.

7Checking what we deliver

  1. Please check what we deliver as soon as you can. If something isn’t as agreed, tell us in writing within 10 working days of delivery, with a short description of the problem.
  2. If we don’t hear from you within that time, or you start using what we delivered, it counts as accepted.

8Subcontractors

  1. We may use subcontractors for parts of the work. We tell you before we do, and we are responsible for their work as if it were our own.

9Who owns what

  1. Each of us keeps what we owned before we started working together.
  2. When you have paid, you get the full right to use what we made for you, including drawings, documentation, configurations and code written for you. You may change it, and you may have others maintain it. We don’t build things you can’t run without us.
  3. We keep the right to our own tools, methods, templates and general know-how, and may use them in other work, without using your confidential information.

10Prices

  1. All prices are in Danish kroner (DKK) and exclude VAT.
  2. A fixed price covers the work described in the offer. If it turns out that the work is substantially different from what was described, we tell you before we continue, and agree what it means for the price.
  3. Work that isn’t at a fixed price is invoiced by the hour at our current rates.
  4. Travel and other expenses are invoiced at cost when the offer says so.
  5. For ongoing agreements, for example running and maintaining a solution, we can adjust prices once a year with at least 30 days’ written notice.

11Invoices and payment

  1. Fixed-price work is invoiced as described in the offer. A two-day analysis is invoiced after the walk-through. Hourly work is invoiced monthly. Ongoing agreements are invoiced monthly in advance.
  2. Invoices are sent by email or as electronic invoices (EAN). Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date.
  3. If payment is late, we may charge interest under the Danish Interest Act (renteloven), and pause the work until the invoice is paid.
  4. If you disagree with an invoice, tell us within 10 working days and say why.

12Defects and delays

  1. If something we delivered doesn’t work as agreed because of us, we fix it, deliver it again, or reduce the price, at our choice. Software is never completely free of errors, and small errors that don’t affect the agreed use are not defects.
  2. Tell us in writing within 10 working days of discovering a defect or delay. Claims expire 12 months after delivery.
  3. Dates in an agreement are estimates, unless the agreement says they are binding.

13Liability

  1. We are liable under the general rules of Danish law, with the limits in this section.
  2. Neither of us is liable for indirect loss, such as lost profit, lost business, lost data or loss of goodwill.
  3. Our total liability under an agreement is limited to what you have paid under that agreement in the 12 months before the damage happened. For a single fixed-price task, it is limited to the price of the task.
  4. We are not liable for third-party software, which is delivered on that supplier’s terms. If it fails, we help you raise it with the supplier.
  5. The limits don’t apply if a loss is caused intentionally or by gross negligence, or by a breach of confidentiality.

14Confidentiality

  1. We both keep the other’s confidential information confidential: anything about the other’s business, customers, prices and processes that we learn while working together, including the content of offers and agreements.
  2. Confidential information may only be shared with employees, subcontractors and advisers who need it, and who are bound by the same duty.
  3. This doesn’t cover information that is already public, that we already knew, or that the law requires us to share.
  4. The duty continues after the agreement ends.
  5. We only mention you as a customer, or describe our work for you, if you have said yes to it first.

15Personal data

  1. We handle personal data as described in our privacy policy.
  2. If we process personal data on your behalf, we sign a data processing agreement with you before we start, and that agreement applies to the processing.
  3. When AI services are part of a solution, we use enterprise services that don’t train on your data.

16Force majeure

  1. Neither of us is responsible for failing to do something because of events outside our control that we could not reasonably have foreseen or avoided, such as natural disasters, war, pandemics, strikes or a breakdown of public infrastructure.
  2. The one affected tells the other as soon as possible. If such an event stops the work for more than 30 working days, either of us can end the agreement with 5 working days’ written notice.

17Ending an agreement

  1. An agreement can be ended as described in the agreement itself.
  2. Either of us can end an agreement straight away if the other breaches it materially and doesn’t put it right within 20 working days of a written notice, or if the other becomes insolvent.
  3. When an agreement ends, you get your data and the documentation for what we built, and we pay or are paid for the work done up to that date.

18Changes to these terms

  1. We may update these general terms. A new version applies to agreements made after it is published. Existing agreements keep the version that was agreed, unless we both agree otherwise.
  2. An agreement can only be changed in writing. Email is fine.

19Law and disputes

  1. Agreements are governed by Danish law.
  2. If we disagree, we first try to solve it by talking. If that doesn’t work, the dispute is settled by the Danish courts, with the court in Aalborg as the first instance.

Questions about these terms? Write to hello@lanes.dk before you sign, and we’ll explain or adjust them in the offer.
